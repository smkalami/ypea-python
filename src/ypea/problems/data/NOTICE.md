# Bundled instance data

## mknap1.txt

Source: OR-Library, J. E. Beasley, multidimensional knapsack problem, file `mknap1`
(https://people.brunel.ac.uk/~mastjjb/jeb/orlib/mknapinfo.html).

The seven problems are those of C. C. Petersen, "Computational experience with variants
of the Balas algorithm applied to the selection of R&D projects", Management Science
13(9), 1967, 736 to 750.
